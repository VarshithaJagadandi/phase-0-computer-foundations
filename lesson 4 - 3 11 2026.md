#### Binary to decimal, by hand

- the idea
    
    each bulb has a price tag. add up the price tags of the bulbs that are On. ignore the Off ones.
    
    - each double the last from left to right, like:
    
    | 128 | 64 | 32 | 16 | 8 | 4 | 2 | 1 |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
- example 1
    1. place values under the bits, starting from the right:
        
        
        | Place value | 8 | 4 | 2 | 1 |
        | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
        | Bit | 1 | 0 | 1 | 1 |
    2. pick the places where the bit is 1: 8, 2, 1
    3. Add them up: 8+2+1 = 11.
- example 2
    
    101101
    
    1. values
    
    | Place value | 32 | 16 | 8 | 4 | 2 | 1 |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    | Bit | 1 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 0 | 1 |
    1. bits with 1 : 32, 8, 4, 1
    2. sum → 32+8+4+1 = 45
- <u>imp</u> <u>points</u> :
    - always start placing values from right. the right-most bit is always 1s. if you start from left, you’ll shift everything and get wrong answer
    - write place values above or below the bits every time. don’t do it in your head util you’re fast.
- shortcut
    
    In your table, 15 was 01111 and 16 was 10000. A single 1 followed by zeros is always a power of two (16, 32, 64...). And a row of all 1s is always **one less** than the next power of two. This will save you time later.
    
- Understanding check
    
    → *what is 1111 decimal? And without adding anything, what do you think 11111 is, and why?*
    
    - so here closest to 1111 is 10000 which is 2^4 = 16, so 1111 is 15.
    - 11111 → closest is 100000 which is 2^5 = 32, so 11111 is 31.
- Micro-exercise
    
    Convert these 10 binary numbers to decimal **by hand** (write the place values under each one). Start easy and go up:
    
    1. 0110
        
        
        | Place value | 8 | 4 | 2 | 1 |
        | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
        | Bit | 0 | 1 | 1 | 0 |
        
        →  4+2 = 6
        
    2. 1010
        
        
        | Place value | 8 | 4 | 2 | 1 |
        | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
        | Bit | 1 | 0 | 1 | 0 |
        
        →  8+2 = 10
        
    3. 1110
        
        
        | Place value | 8 | 4 | 2 | 1 |
        | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
        | Bit | 1 | 1 | 1 | 0 |
        
        →  8+4+2 = 14
        
    4. 10101
        
        
        | Place value | 16 | 8 | 4 | 2 | 1 |
        | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
        | Bit | 1 | 0 | 1 | 0 | 1 |
        
        →  16+4+1 = 21
        
    5. 11111
        
        
        | Place value | 16 | 8 | 4 | 2 | 1 |
        | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
        | Bit | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
        
        →  16+8+4+2+1 = 31
        
    6. 100000
        
        
        | Place value | 32 | 16 | 8 | 4 | 2 | 1 |
        | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
        | Bit | 1 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

        | Place value | 32 | 16 | 8 | 4 | 2 | 1 |
