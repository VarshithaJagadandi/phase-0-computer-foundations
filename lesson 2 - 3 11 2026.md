### **Bits and Bytes**

- Bit
    - A bit is one switch, one bulb, it is either 0 or 1.
    - it can say only “yes” or “no”, on it’s own, like single bulb for one car.
- Byte
    - a byte is 8 bits together, like a row of 8 bulbs.
    - Using formula, 8 bulbs can make 2^8 = 256 different patterns.
    - enough to give a number to every English letter(caps and small) and every digit, and many symbols.
    - letters usually take 1 byte

→ sweety has 6 letters → about 6 bytes → 48 bits or switches.

- why do we need bits at all ?
    
    think of eggs, you can buy 1 egg, but shops sell a dozen because it’s handy size. A byte in the computer’s “dozen” : a handy-sized group of bits, so everyone agrees on one unit

- understanding check
    
    *→ how many different cars could 3 bulbs represent, and how many bulbs would you need for 16 cars?*
    
    3 bulbs → 3 bits → 2^3 → 8 cars can be represented
    
    16 cars → 16 = 2^4 → 4 bits → 4 bulbs are need
