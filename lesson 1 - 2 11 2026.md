#### ***Why computers only use 0 and 1***

- Light Switch
    
    it only requires 2 positions: On or Off
    
    no “half full ” or “half empty” → makes easier to tell the state. → clear answers
    
    <u>similarly</u> :
    
    computer is build of billions of tiny switches. → sooo.. tiny → controlled by electricity → transistors.
    
    - On (electricity flows) - 1
    - Off (no flow of electricity) - 0
    
    *Computer only understands 0 and 1*  →  *Computer is made of switches that are On or Off*
    
- why not normal counting ?
    
    electricity inside computer can wobble due to heat, noise, small changes → but with 2 states, a comp can tell On from Off very reliably, billions of times per second
    
    <u>reasons</u> :
    
    - simple to build → 2 positions → easy and cheap
    - reliable → On or Off is much harder to get wrong compared to 10 different levels
    - fast → can flip billions of times per second
- one switch - many switches
    
    one switch → yes or no. but, when:
    
    - 1 - 2 possibilities - (0, 1)
    - 2 - 4 possibilities - (00, 01, 10, 11)
    - 3 - 8 possibilities
    - and so on…
    
    each new switch doubles the number of things you can represent → *numbers, letters, colors, songs, photos, videos*
    
- real-world example
    
    when we take photo, phone doesn’t store “*picture*”. it stores huge list of numbers (like how bright each tiny dot is), and those numbers are stored as 0s and 1s. when you open the photo, the phone reads the 0s and 1s and turns them back into picture you can see. the picture is just a translation of the switches.
    
- quick understanding
    
    *→ why would a computer designer choose “ON or OFF” instead of 10 different electricity levels?*
    
    so, let’s take 2 bulbs - b1 and b2 and then 2 cars - c1 and c2. now, when c1 arrives the light b1 is turned on and when c2 arrives c2 is turned on, and then another car c3 arrives how do we represent it? it we would need more light bulbs or else the representation of quantity of electricity levels must be increased - but instead we can just do (0,0) for c1 , (0,1) for c2 , (1,0) for c3 and then (1,1) for c4 car. in this manner we can be able to represent 2 power n number of cars , here n = no. of bulbs. here the main part is that the On and Off make it harder to get wrong compared to the 10 different level one, and faster and simple to build.
